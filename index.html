Questions shop owners ask

What do I need to use Kashera? +

An Android phone or tablet and an internet connection. You don't need to buy a till or any special hardware.

What happens after the 14-day trial? +

You can subscribe for R249 a month to keep using the Pro features: reports, stock, recipes and staff accounts. If you don't subscribe, you keep the free features.

Does Kashera take card payments for me? +

Kashera records whether a sale was paid in cash or by card, so your numbers are right. It doesn't process card payments itself yet. Pay-by-bank QR payments are on our roadmap.

Can my staff use their own phones? +

Yes. Connect each device to your store, and every staff member logs in with their own 4-digit PIN. Sales are recorded under their name.

Can I give my customers a receipt? +

Yes. Tick "Slip" at checkout and Kashera creates a PDF receipt with your shop name, items, tax and total, which you can share straight to the customer.

How do I install the APK? +

Tap Download APK, open the file, and follow the prompts. Android may ask you to allow installs from unknown sources, which is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.

💡

Android Installation Note:

When installing the APK file directly, enable "Install from unknown sources" in your Android security settings if prompted.
