Kashera POS is a simple point-of-sale and business management system designed for small food businesses that want to keep better records of sales, stock and daily operations using an Android phone or tablet.

Running a food business involves more than serving customers and collecting money. Owners also need to know what they sold, which ingredients they used, what stock remains and how the business is performing. A POS system can help bring these records together.

What does a POS system do?

A point-of-sale (POS) system helps a business record transactions and manage sales information. Depending on its features, it may also help owners monitor inventory, review reports, manage staff access and keep customer credit records.

Who can use Kashera POS?

Kashera POS is designed for a range of small food businesses, including:

  • Takeaways and small restaurants
  • Food trucks and mobile kitchens
  • Food stalls and market food vendors
  • Kota shops and similar takeaway food businesses
  • Small food shops that need clearer sales and stock records

Track sales and review business activity

Recording sales helps owners review how much the business sells over a period and identify popular menu items. Kashera POS provides sales recording and sales reports so owners can review business activity instead of relying only on memory.

Manage ingredients and stock

Food businesses use ingredients every day. Kashera POS supports ingredient stock tracking and recipe setup. When menu items are linked to their ingredients and quantities, the system can deduct the configured ingredient amounts as those items are sold.

Correct setup matters: stock deductions depend on the ingredients and quantities configured for each menu item.

Keep track of staff activity and customer credit

Kashera POS includes staff and cashier access controls, customer credit records and sales reporting. These features can help owners maintain clearer records of business activity and outstanding customer balances.

Use a phone or tablet instead of specialised till hardware

Kashera POS runs on Android phones and tablets. This gives eligible small food businesses an option to manage sales and records using a compatible device rather than buying a dedicated POS terminal. Kashera is currently an Android app, not an iPhone app.
